Assetto Corsa models, as glTF 2.0

assetto-corsa-gltf reads the .kn5 files Assetto Corsa ships its cars and tracks in and writes glTF 2.0 - a .gltf with textures beside it, or a single self-contained .glb. The node hierarchy comes across one for one, liveries resolve to the paint the game would actually use, and the shader parameters become PBR materials.

What it does

Cars and tracks

A car is one .kn5. A track is several, placed by its models_*.ini - point at the ini and they arrive in one glTF, positioned. Magione converts to 2.38 million triangles across 963 meshes.

Cars · Tracks

The hierarchy survives

A car carries around 250 nodes with no geometry on them: wheel centres, suspension pickups, the steering column, door hinges - named and positioned. Flatten the tree and you throw away the useful part.

How nodes map

Liveries, and the right paint

An Assetto Corsa livery lives outside the model, and on most Kunos road cars the colour is not even in the diffuse - it is a flat detail map the shader multiplies over a shared grey sheet. That is why naive imports arrive white.

Liveries and paint

glTF or a single GLB

--glb folds the geometry and every texture into one file for a viewer or a pipeline. Leave it off for .gltf + .bin + loose PNGs when you intend to work on the textures.

The output format

PBR materials, derived not guessed

Specular exponent and intensity become roughness; fresnelMaxLevel becomes KHR_materials_specular; car paint's second, sharper sun lobe becomes KHR_materials_clearcoat, which is what makes paint read as paint.

What comes out

Converting the MX-5 ND at top detail, with its first livery:

$ kn5-to-gltf ks_mazda_mx5_nd/mazda_mx5_lod_a.kn5 out --name mx5 --glb
skin   : 00_soul_red_metallic (3 textures)
nodes  : 323  (153 transforms, 169 meshes, 0 empty, 12 variants dropped)
tris   : 168283
images : 61   materials: 55
folded : 1 texture names that differed only in case
glb    : 12.0 MB
wrote  : out/mx5.glb

153 of those 323 nodes carry no geometry at all, and they are the reason to keep the tree. Twelve meshes were dropped: the runtime _BLUR wheels, the _DAMAGE shells, and the low-resolution half of the in-file cockpit and steering-wheel LOD pairs - all things the game swaps at runtime and a static glTF cannot.

Install

pip install assetto-corsa-gltf

Python 3.9 or newer. Pillow is the only dependency; everything else is the standard library. Works on Windows, macOS and Linux, and you do not need Assetto Corsa installed to build or test it.

Three commands land on your PATH: kn5-to-gltf, kn5-studio, kn5-survey.

Known issues and limits

  • CSP-encrypted models are refused. The plain section of an encrypted .kn5 holds decoys - 1×1 textures and 6 cm cubes - so converting one would quietly produce a car with boxes for wheels.
  • No animation, no skinning. Skinned meshes come across as static geometry in bind pose; the pivots they move around are present as nodes.
  • No physics data. data.acd is encrypted and untouched. A track's surface table is the exception.
  • Track output is large. No decimation and no Draco - Magione is a 165 MB buffer. Run it through gltf-transform if you need it smaller.
  • Materials are an approximation. Assetto Corsa is a Blinn-Phong-era renderer; the conversions that exist are arithmetic, and the ones that do not are left out rather than faked.
